# SEO audit, 14 September 2026

This is a read-only audit of the Intelligent Carpet Cleaning public site, the Astro source under `site/` (26 URLs in the last local build). It covers technical and on-page SEO from source and ends with a LocalBusiness JSON-LD draft built from the code's business facts. There was no live crawl, no browser run and no Search Console access. The 10 September local build in `site/dist` is used as supporting evidence for canonicals, the sitemap and link forms; the only source file changed since that build is `privacy.astro` (git log), so the build is representative for structure. Nothing here has been applied to the code.

RAG key: Red blocks indexing or ranking, or is a compliance exposure. Amber costs something measurable, fix before or soon after launch. Green is in order, no action.

## 1. Executive summary

The site is structurally sound for a local service business: static HTML per page, one layout carrying title, description, canonical, Open Graph and a site-wide LocalBusiness block, breadcrumb and FAQ markup on the templated pages, a generated sitemap, an honest robots.txt, and security headers including HSTS. Content is unusually careful about claims for a site at this stage.

It is also, by design, invisible. Every response carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and every canonical points at a domain that does not yet serve the site. Both are correct pre-launch choices and both are flagged in the code; the risk is entirely at cutover, where forgetting either one keeps the real site out of the index.

Top priorities:

1. **Cutover checklist (Red, by design).** Remove the pre-launch header block, confirm `www.intelligentclean.co.uk` serves the site, submit the sitemap, and test-call the phone number before the Google Business Profile goes live.
2. **Trailing-slash consistency (Amber).** Canonicals and the sitemap use `/about/`; nearly every internal link uses `/about`. One config flag and a link sweep.
3. **NAP locality (Amber).** The site says "based in Cheltenham" and the JSON-LD says `addressLocality: Cheltenham`; the verified profile address will be in the Gloucester core area (D-016). Decide the public locality once, before the profile is verified.
4. **Metadata length on the two specialist pages (Amber).** Titles of 85 and 76 characters, descriptions of 222 and 177.
5. **Article markup and sitemap dates (Amber).** Guides carry `dateModified` but no `datePublished` or `image`; the sitemap has no `lastmod` although every guide and area page has an `updated` date.

Quick wins: `lang="en-GB"`, `og:type="article"` on guides, `og:locale`, a real favicon instead of the 76 KB JPEG, a BreadcrumbList on the two specialist pages, and a second inbound link to `/history` and the specialist pages.

### T1. Site-wide noindex, nofollow (Red, by design)

`netlify.toml:158-161` serves `X-Robots-Tag = "noindex, nofollow"` for `/*`, so nothing on the site can be indexed while the block stands. It is the single control that decides whether the site exists in search. The block is marked `TODO(prelaunch/noindex)` at `netlify.toml:154` and sits on the hardening checklist at `CLAUDE.md:274`, so it is known; the risk is purely that it outlives the cutover.

At cutover, delete the block and confirm with `curl -I https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/` that the header is gone. Leave `robots.txt` alone: `site/public/robots.txt:13` keeps `Allow: /` on purpose so crawlers can fetch the page and see the header at all. Standard: Google Search Central, robots meta and X-Robots-Tag.

### T2. Canonical target does not serve the site yet (Red, by design)

`site/astro.config.mjs:9` sets `site: 'https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk'`, so every canonical (`BaseLayout.astro:41`), `og:url` and sitemap `<loc>` points there, and the comment at `netlify.toml:145` records that the domain currently serves a 123 Reg parking page. Until DNS moves the canonical cannot be honoured; once it moves, this is exactly right.

Nothing to change in code. At cutover: DNS to Netlify, custom domain primary, `www` as the canonical host with the apex redirecting to it (or the reverse, but pick one and match `site:`), then submit `https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/sitemap-index.xml` in Search Console.

### T3. Trailing-slash mismatch between links and canonicals (Amber)

The built canonicals and sitemap use the directory form (`/about/`, `/areas/cheltenham/`) because `BaseLayout.astro:41` derives the canonical from `Astro.url.pathname` under the default `build.format: 'directory'`. The nav (`Nav.astro:9-16`), footer, CTAs and content links use the bare form (`/about`, `/book`). In the built output there are 86 links to `/book`, 66 to `/areas` and 59 to `/services`, none with a trailing slash, and the guide-to-guide links use both forms (`/guides/cleaning-wool-carpets` and `/guides/cleaning-wool-carpets/` each appear twice).

So every internal link resolves to a URL that is not the canonical URL. On Netlify a request for `/about` where `/about/index.html` exists is normally answered with a 301 to `/about/`, which would put a redirect hop in front of every internal link. That is a crawl-efficiency cost and a mixed signal rather than an indexing failure, and the live redirect behaviour is unverified because this audit ran no crawl.

The fix is `trailingSlash: 'always'` in `astro.config.mjs` and a sweep of the `href="/..."` values in `site/src` to the slashed form (the root `/` is already fine). Add a build-time check that every internal `href` in `site/dist` ends in `/` or points at a file, so the two forms cannot drift apart again. The sitemap integration follows the `trailingSlash` setting. Standard: Google Search Central on canonicalisation; the seo-audit checklist item on trailing-slash consistency.

### T4. Sitemap has no lastmod (Amber)

`site/dist/sitemap-0.xml` has 26 `<loc>` entries and no `<lastmod>` elements, although every guide and area page carries an `updated` field (`site/src/content.config.ts:16` and `:44`). The cost is small: Google uses `lastmod` when it is consistently accurate, and without it recrawl of an updated guide is slower.

Pass a `serialize` function to `@astrojs/sitemap` that reads `updated` for collection pages and the git commit date (or a build date) for the static pages. Only ship it if the dates are real. A `lastmod` that changes on every build is worse than none.

### T5. Document language (Amber, minor)

`BaseLayout.astro:74` declares `<html lang="en">`. The content is British English for a UK audience, `en-GB` is the accurate tag, and Bing uses `<html lang>` as a weak regional signal (seo-audit, international section). Change it to `lang="en-GB"` and add `<meta property="og:locale" content="en_GB" />` at the same time.

### T6. Favicon and icons (Amber, minor)

`BaseLayout.astro:81` uses `/logo.jpg` (900 by 491, 76,675 bytes) as the icon, and there is no PNG or SVG favicon, no `apple-touch-icon` and no web manifest. Ranking impact is nil; the cost is 76 KB on every first visit and a cropped rectangle where Google shows a square icon in mobile results. A 32 and 180 pixel PNG (or an SVG) from the mark, plus `apple-touch-icon`, closes it.

### T7. Thinly linked pages (Amber)

In the built output `/history` has one inbound link (`site/src/pages/guides/index.astro:33`), and `/services/decontamination-fogging` and `/services/pressure-washing` have one each (`site/src/pages/services.astro:118` and `:124`). None of the three is in the nav or footer. All three sit within three clicks of the home page, so they are not orphans, but a single link is a weak signal for two pages that target their own queries ("pressure washing Cheltenham", "fogging Gloucestershire").

Link the two specialist pages from the home services grid (`index.astro:84-89`) and the area-page service list (`areas/[slug].astro:69-74`), and link `/history` from the guides it relates to. The nav can stay as it is.

### T8. robots.txt (Green)

`site/public/robots.txt:13` allows all crawling and `:15` names `https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/sitemap-index.xml`, which matches the generated index file. The comment explains why `Disallow` would defeat the pre-launch header. Correct.

### T9. Utility pages excluded (Green)

`/booking-action` carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` (`site/src/pages/booking-action.astro:24`) and is filtered from the sitemap (`site/astro.config.mjs:12`); `/admin*` gets `X-Robots-Tag = "noindex, nofollow"` and `no-store` (`netlify.toml:131-135`). Correct.

### T10. Security headers and HTTPS (Green)

`netlify.toml:121-128`: `X-Frame-Options`, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy` and `Strict-Transport-Security` with a one-year max-age. HTTPS is Netlify-default. The CSP omission is documented and is a security item, not an SEO one.

### T11. Performance structure (Green, unmeasured)

Core Web Vitals cannot be measured from source. The structure is favourable: no client-side framework on the marketing pages, fonts self-hosted with pinned Latin subsets and only the used weights (`BaseLayout.astro:21-29`), the home hero served through `astro:assets` as WebP with `loading="eager"` and `fetchpriority="high"` (`index.astro:52-61`), and per-page scoped CSS. The one heavy page is `/book`, which carries the chat client as an inline script; that is the application page and its INP should be measured after launch rather than guessed at.

### T12. URL structure (Green)

Lowercase, hyphenated, descriptive, one level per section (`/areas/cheltenham/`, `/guides/cleaning-wool-carpets/`, `/services/pressure-washing/`). No parameters, no dates in paths.

The seo-audit target is 50 to 60 characters. The two specialist titles (`decontamination-fogging.astro:48`, `pressure-washing.astro:44`) put the town names and the full brand in one string, and the guides gain 30 characters from the `| Intelligent Carpet Cleaning` suffix applied at `guides/[slug].astro:71`.

For the guides, shorten the suffix (`| ICC Guides`, say) or drop it on guides only; the brand is already carried by the publisher markup and the site name.

### O2. Meta description length (Amber)

`decontamination-fogging.astro:49` is 222 characters, `terms.astro:19` is 186, `pressure-washing.astro:45` is 177, `diy-vs-professional-carpet-cleaning.md` is 164. Google truncates around 155 to 160. Trim each to one sentence of service plus area plus call to action; the fogging description can lose the certification clause, which the page body already carries.

### O3. Home page H1 (Amber)

`index.astro:35` renders `Smarter Cleaning. Done Properly.` The primary query (carpet cleaning, Cheltenham, Gloucestershire) appears in the title (`index.astro:27`) and the sub-heading (`index.astro:36`) but not in the H1. A brand line is a legitimate choice for a home page; the cost is a weaker on-page signal for the one page most likely to rank for the head term. A middle path is `Carpet cleaning in Cheltenham and Gloucestershire, done properly.` with the brand line kept as the eyebrow.

### O4. Heading semantics (Amber, minor)

- The home page feature titles (`index.astro:74-76`) and service card titles (`index.astro:85-88`) are `<div class="feature-title">` and `<div class="service-card-title">`. The page therefore has an H1 and two H2s and nothing else, so the four services are invisible to the outline. Make the card titles `<h3>`.
- `services.astro:27` and `:115` are both H2s reading "Specialist Cleaning Services" and "Specialist cleaning services". The second section is the decontamination and pressure-washing block; give it a distinct heading ("Decontamination and exterior cleaning").

### O5. Open Graph and social cards (Amber, minor)

`BaseLayout.astro:84` sets `og:type="website"` on every page including the eight guides, which are articles. `BaseLayout.astro:86` and `:91` use the 900 by 491 logo on every page; the common recommendation for large cards is 1200 by 630, and a logo on a white field is a weak preview. Add an `ogType` prop defaulting to `website` with `article` on guides, and a 1200 by 630 share image once real photography exists (see O7). Add `og:locale`.

### O6. NAP consistency (Amber)

The phone number is consistent. `01452 452356` appears on the site pages (`contact.astro:13`, `book.astro:69`, `privacy.astro:31`, `terms.astro:30`), in the assistant prompt (`chat.js:76`), in the email sign-off (`handoffs.js:166`) and in the JSON-LD as `+441452452356` (`BaseLayout.astro:54`). Two loose ends remain: `CLAUDE.md:289` (the developer guide, not code, so nothing is served from it) still gives the superseded `01242 279590`, and the number has not yet been test-called (NEXT_SESSION.md, "test-call 01452 452356 once Tamar allocates"). A published number that does not ring is the worst NAP outcome there is, so the test call belongs on the cutover checklist ahead of the profile.

The locality is not consistent. The public copy says "Based in Cheltenham" (`about.astro:25`, `contact.astro:19`), the email sign-off says "Cheltenham, Gloucestershire" (`handoffs.js:166`) and the JSON-LD says `addressLocality: 'Cheltenham'` (`BaseLayout.astro:58`), while D-016 records that the registered address used for profile verification is in the Gloucester core area (GL3). Google ranks a service-area business on the verified address, so this costs no proximity ranking, but a profile showing Gloucester beside a website saying Cheltenham is an avoidable inconsistency. Decide one public locality (or say "Cheltenham and Gloucester", which `terms.astro:30` already does) and apply it to the three surfaces and the JSON-LD together. Standard: Google Business Profile guidelines for service-area businesses, and D-016 in DECISIONS.md.

### O7. E-E-A-T (Amber)

The guides are authored and published as the Organization (`guides/[slug].astro:38-43`); Mark's name appears only on `/terms` and `/privacy`; the About page says "over 15 years of professional carpet cleaning experience" (`about.astro:24`) without saying whose; there are no photographs of real work anywhere on the site (the open A2 item in NEXT_SESSION.md). For a one-person local trade, the person is the expertise signal. Add a short named operator section on `/about` carrying the experience figure and the fogging certification (in the wording D-038 pins), and add real job photography when Mark supplies it. This also fixes the share image in O5.

### O8. Titles, descriptions and H1s (Green)

All 26 URLs have unique titles and descriptions. Every page renders exactly one H1. Headings are not used for styling.

### O9. Internal linking (Green)

The home page links every area page and the areas index (`index.astro:99`, `:107`), the guides cross-link each other with descriptive anchors, and every content page ends with a CTA to `/book`. Nav and footer are compact.

### O10. Images (Green)

The two content images have alt text (`index.astro:54`, `book.astro:42`). The hero image is responsive WebP with dimensions emitted. There is little else to audit because the site has almost no imagery (O7).

### O11. Keyword overlap (Info)

The home page and `/areas/cheltenham/` both target "carpet cleaning Cheltenham". This is normal for a Cheltenham-based business and not a defect; watch the two in Search Console after launch and, if they swap positions, let the home page own the head term and point the Cheltenham page at neighbourhood and postcode queries.

### O12. Content gap (Info, long term)

Residential, commercial, stain treatment and upholstery live as anchored sections on `/services/`. The two enquiry-led specialist services got their own pages; the four core services did not. "Upholstery cleaning Cheltenham" and "commercial carpet cleaning Gloucester" are separate query families that a section anchor cannot rank for. Candidate pages: `/services/upholstery-cleaning/`, `/services/stain-treatment/`, `/services/commercial/`, each with the existing price lines rendered from `pricing.js` and a Service block tied to `#business`.

### S1. LocalBusiness (Green)

`BaseLayout.astro:46-71` emits one LocalBusiness on every page with `@id` `https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/#business`, name, description, url, image, telephone, email, a partial PostalAddress, `areaServed`, `openingHoursSpecification` generated from `tradingHours.openingHoursSpecification()` (`BaseLayout.astro:69`, `tradingHours.js:235-243`) and `priceRange`. The Service blocks on the area and specialist pages reference the same `@id` (`areas/[slug].astro:51`, `decontamination-fogging.astro:18`) rather than redeclaring the business. This is the right shape. The draft in section 5 extends it.

### S2. Article markup (Amber)

`guides/[slug].astro:30-44` emits `Article` with `headline`, `description`, `url`, `mainEntityOfPage`, `dateModified`, `author` and `publisher`. The schema skill lists `headline`, `image`, `datePublished` and `author` as required; Google's Article documentation lists `image` and `datePublished` as recommended. Both are absent. `publisher` (`:39-43`) is a bare Organization rather than a reference to `#business`, so the same entity is declared twice with different shapes.

Fix: add `published` to the guides frontmatter schema (`content.config.ts:11-23`) and emit `datePublished`; emit `image` (the share image from O5); set `author` and `publisher` to `{ '@id': '.../#business' }`.

### S3. Breadcrumbs and areaServed shape (Amber, minor)

Area pages and guides emit BreadcrumbList; the two specialist pages do not, and their `areaServed` is the string `'Gloucestershire'` (`pressure-washing.astro:15`) where the area template uses `{ '@type': 'City', name: town }` (`areas/[slug].astro:52`). Add `Home > Services > Pressure Washing` breadcrumbs and use `{ '@type': 'AdministrativeArea', name: 'Gloucestershire' }` so the two templates agree.

### S4. areaServed not single-sourced (Amber)

`BaseLayout.astro:62-65` lists ten place names by hand, including `'Bishops Cleeve'` where `serviceArea.js:23` spells it `Bishop's Cleeve`. D-006 makes `shared/config` the single source, and the areas collection already drives the home page strip. Derive the list from `serviceArea.core_towns`, `serviceArea.wider_towns` and the `areas` collection (section 5 shows the result).

### S5. FAQPage (Info)

Six area pages and eight guides emit FAQPage that mirrors the visible FAQ. Since August 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government and health sites, so no rich result should be expected. The markup still matches page content and helps entity and AI-search understanding; keep it, expect nothing from it in the SERP.

### S6. Absent properties (Info)

`sameAs` (no live Google Business Profile or social URLs in the code), `geo` (D-016: home-based service-area business, address withheld) and `aggregateRating` (D-015: no verified reviews yet) are all correctly absent. Add `sameAs` with the profile URL the day it is live.
