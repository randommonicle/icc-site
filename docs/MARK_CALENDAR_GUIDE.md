# Sharing your calendar with Intelligent Clean

**Why this matters (30 seconds).** The new Intelligent Clean website can take
bookings on its own. For it to never book a cleaning job on top of one of your
Regency jobs or a personal appointment, it needs to see when you are already
busy. This one setup lets it do that. It only ever sees *that* you are busy at a
time, never what the appointment is or who it is with. No personal detail leaves
your calendar.

**You do this once. It takes about two minutes.**

---

## Important: this has to be done on a computer, not the phone

The Google Calendar phone app **cannot** share a calendar. The option simply is
not in the app, which is why the earlier steps led nowhere. Sharing only exists
in Google Calendar on a computer, in a web browser.

If you only have your phone, there is a workaround at the bottom, but a computer
is much easier. Use one if you can.

---

## The steps (on a computer)

1. Open a web browser and go to **calendar.google.com**.
2. **Check which account you are in.** Look at the coloured circle at the very
   top-right. It must be **talktoregency@gmail.com** (the Regency diary). If it
   is a different account, click the circle, then **Add another account** or
   switch to **talktoregency@gmail.com** and sign in. This only works from the
   account that owns the diary.
3. On the left, under **My calendars**, find the calendar called
   **talktoregency** (or your name). Rest the mouse on it. Three dots (**⋮**)
   appear on the right of it. Click them.
4. Click **Settings and sharing**.
5. Scroll down to **Share with specific people or groups**.
6. Click **Add people and groups**.
7. Paste this address exactly (Ben will send it to you so you can copy it):

   **icc-booking-bot@icc-calendar-507721.iam.gserviceaccount.com**

8. In the permission dropdown next to it, choose **See only free/busy (hide
   details)**.
9. Click **Send**.

**That's it.** When that address shows in the "Share with specific people or
groups" list with **See only free/busy** beside it, you are finished. You never
have to do this again. It will not send you any confirmation, and that is
normal.

## Two quick "what ifs"

**If you keep Regency jobs in more than one calendar.** In step 3 you might see
more than one calendar on the left. If any of them hold things that would clash
with cleaning work, do steps 3 to 9 again for each one, and tell Ben which ones
you shared so he can point the system at them.

**If you cannot get to a computer.** Do not rely on the phone's "Desktop site"
mode. It does not work properly for this. Ask Ben to do this part for you
instead.

**If you get stuck.** If anything looks different from the above, do not worry.
Take a screenshot and send it to Ben. Nothing you tap on this screen can break
anything.
