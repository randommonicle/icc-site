# Sharing your calendar with Intelligent Clean

**Why this matters (30 seconds).** The new Intelligent Clean website can take
bookings on its own. For it to never book a cleaning job on top of one of your
Regency jobs or a personal appointment, it needs to see when you are already
busy. This one setup lets it do that. It only ever sees *that* you are busy at a
time, never what the appointment is or who it is with. No personal detail leaves
your phone.

**You do this once. It takes about two minutes.**

---

## First: open the RIGHT app

Your Samsung has two calendar apps. You must use **Google Calendar** (the round
icon with a coloured **"31"**). Do **not** use **Samsung Calendar** (a different
icon). The sharing setting only exists in the Google Calendar app.

If you cannot find Google Calendar, open the **Play Store**, search
**Google Calendar**, install it, then come back here.

---

## The steps

1. Open the **Google Calendar** app.
2. Tap the **three lines** (☰) at the top-left.
3. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.
4. Tap your calendar in the list (it is usually your **name** or your **email
   address**).
5. Tap **Add people or groups**.
6. Type this exactly, then tap it when it appears:
   **ben@intelligentclean.co.uk**
7. Set the permission to **See only free/busy (hide details)**. If it shows
   anything else, tap it and pick that one.
8. Tap **Send** (or **Save**) at the top.

**That's it.** When the screen goes back and shows *ben@intelligentclean.co.uk*
in the list, you are finished. You never have to do this again.

---

## Two quick "what ifs"

**If you keep appointments in more than one calendar.** In step 4 you might see
more than one calendar (for example a separate personal one). If any of them
hold things that would clash with cleaning work, do steps 4 to 8 again for each
one. If there is only one, ignore this.

**If you get stuck.** If step 5 has no "Add people or groups", or anything looks
different from the above, do not worry. Take a screenshot and send it to Ben.
Nothing you tap on this screen can break anything.

---

cleaning jobs show up alongside everything else. There is nothing for you to do
for that part.*
