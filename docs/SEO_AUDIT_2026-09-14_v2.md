## 1. Scope and method

What was examined, so the findings can be re-derived:

- **Source.** Every page template in `site/src/pages/`, the layout, the two components, the two content collections (six area pages, eight guides), `site/astro.config.mjs`, `site/public/robots.txt`, and `netlify.toml`. Business facts were taken from `shared/config/*.js` and the `BUSINESS DETAILS` block of the assistant prompt in `server/netlify/functions/chat.js`, which CLAUDE.md names as the business-facts source.
- **Built output.** The main checkout holds a `site/dist` built on 10 September 2026 (`C:\Users\bengr\Projects\ICC\icc-site\site\dist`). It predates one source change, the 13 September `privacy.astro` copy edit, which does not affect anything measured here. Titles, descriptions, canonicals, H1s, sitemap entries, JSON-LD blocks and internal link targets were read from this build, not inferred from templates.
- **Live host.** Read-only requests to `https://super-frangollo-c3a14a.netlify.app` on 14 September 2026 for status codes, redirects and response headers, plus a DNS lookup of the production domain through 8.8.8.8. Live phone number, trading hours and the privacy "Last updated" date were checked against source and match, so the live site is the audited source.
- **JSON-LD detection.** The `seo-audit` skill warns that fetch-and-strip tools miss structured data. Here it was read two ways: from the built HTML, and from the rendered DOM in the in-app browser (`document.querySelectorAll('script[type="application/ld+json"]')`). Both agree.
- **Not done.** No Search Console or analytics access exists yet (the site is pre-launch and noindexed). PageSpeed Insights was attempted and refused: the anonymous API quota was exhausted for the day, so no Lighthouse run appears here. The single timing sample in section 4 is one in-app browser load, not a measurement. No keyword research, competitor SERP analysis or backlink data was gathered.

RAG convention: **Red** blocks indexing or launch; **Amber** should be fixed before launch or in the first weeks after; **Green** is sound, or minor. Each finding names the standard or source it rests on.

## 2. Summary and priorities

The foundations are good for a site of this size. It is static, ships no JavaScript on any content page, self-hosts two fonts with `font-display: swap`, serves a responsive WebP hero image with explicit dimensions, carries a self-referencing canonical, unique title and description on every page, exactly one H1 per page, an XML sitemap, security headers and HTTPS. Structured data is already present on every page (`LocalBusiness`) and on the area and guide pages (`BreadcrumbList`, `Service`, `Article`, `FAQPage`), and it is generated from the same config the booking engine uses, which is the right design.

Nothing on the site is indexable today, by design: a site-wide `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` header is served while the site sits on the temporary Netlify host, and every canonical points at `www.intelligentclean.co.uk`, which currently has no DNS record. The audit therefore splits into what must happen at cutover and what should be fixed independently of it.

Five priorities, in order:

1. **Cutover sequence (Red, by design).** DNS to Netlify, remove the `noindex` header block, verify with `curl`, submit the sitemap. The runbook exists (`docs/LAUNCH_CUTOVER.md` steps 2 to 4); this audit adds three checks to it (section 10).
2. **Trailing-slash mismatch (Amber).** Canonicals and the sitemap use `/about/`; the navigation, footer and cards link to `/about`; Netlify answers `/about` with a 301 to `/about/`. Every internal click on the site is a redirect hop. One config change plus a link sweep fixes it.
3. **Locality and NAP consistency (Amber).** The site, the assistant prompt and the `LocalBusiness` block say the business is based in Cheltenham; the decision records place the base and the Google Business Profile verification address in Gloucester (GL3). This needs a decision before the Business Profile is verified, not a code fix.
4. **Structured data gaps (Amber).** `Article` blocks lack `image` and `datePublished`; the specialist service pages have no `BreadcrumbList`; the sitemap has no `lastmod` although every guide and area carries an `updated` date; the `LocalBusiness` block has no `sameAs` or `logo`. Section 9 drafts the replacement `LocalBusiness` block from the code's own facts.
5. **Quick wins (Green to Amber).** Six titles and four descriptions over the working limits (two of each by a wide margin), a non-square JPEG favicon, no long-cache header on hashed assets, Netlify's default 404 page, and a home-page H1 with no service or place term.

## 3. Crawlability and indexation

### T-01. Site-wide noindex, nofollow header (Red, intentional, cutover item)

- **Issue.** Every response, including `robots.txt` and the sitemap, carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`.
- **Impact.** Total. Nothing is indexed until the block is removed. Leaving it in place after the domain cutover would keep the real site out of the index entirely; CLAUDE.md already names this as the first thing to check if nothing appears in Search Console.
- **Evidence.** `netlify.toml:158-161`; live header on `/` on 14 September 2026: `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`. `robots.txt` deliberately keeps `Allow: /` so crawlers can fetch pages and see the header (`site/public/robots.txt:1-13`), which is the correct combination.
- **Fix.** Delete the block flagged `TODO(prelaunch/noindex)` at cutover, redeploy, and verify with `curl -sI https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/` (no `X-Robots-Tag`) and `curl -sI https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk/admin` (still `noindex, nofollow`, from the separate `/admin*` rule at `netlify.toml:131-135`).
- **Priority.** Cutover step. Standard: Google Search Central, "Robots meta tag and X-Robots-Tag specifications".

### T-02. Canonical, og:url, sitemap and robots all point at a host with no DNS (Red, pre-launch state)

- **Issue.** `astro.config.mjs:9` sets `site` to `https://www.intelligentclean.co.uk`, so every canonical, `og:url`, sitemap `<loc>` and the `Sitemap:` line in `robots.txt:15` names that host. On 14 September 2026 neither `intelligentclean.co.uk` nor `www.intelligentclean.co.uk` returned an A record via 8.8.8.8 (nameservers `ns55/ns56.domaincontrol.com`; the zone has NS and SOA records only).
- **Impact.** None today, because of T-01. At cutover it becomes the whole point: the canonical host must resolve to Netlify before the noindex comes off, or crawlers will be told the canonical is a dead host.
- **Evidence.** The `netlify.toml:145` comment says the domain "today serves a 123 Reg parking page". That is no longer what is served; the name does not resolve at all. Report what is observed, and update the comment when the block is deleted.
- **Fix.** `docs/LAUNCH_CUTOVER.md` step 2 (apex `A`, `www` `CNAME`, `www` primary in Netlify so the apex 301s to `www`). The code already emits `www`, so no source change is needed for the host. The `astro.config.mjs:5-7` comment calling the value "a placeholder until the domain is chosen (D-013)" is stale: D-013 was resolved on 7 June 2026. One-line housekeeping.
- **Priority.** Cutover step.

### T-03. Trailing-slash mismatch between links, canonicals and the host (Amber)

- **Issue.** Astro builds directory-style output (`/about/index.html`), so canonicals and sitemap entries are `/about/`. The navigation and footer link to `/about`, `/services`, `/areas`, `/guides`, `/book`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`; every card and CTA does the same. Netlify answers the slash-less form with a 301 to the slash form.
- **Impact.** Every internal link on the site is a redirect hop. Google follows 301s and consolidates signals through them, so this is not a penalty; it is crawl waste, a slower first hop for every click, and an inconsistency that Search Console will report as "Page with redirect" for the linked forms. On a brand-new domain with no history, a clean internal link graph is worth having from day one.
- **Evidence.** Live: `GET /about` returns 301 to `/about/`; `/areas/cheltenham` returns 301 to `/areas/cheltenham/`. Built HTML: `href="/about"` appears once on each of the 26 pages, `href="/book"` 86 times, `href="/areas"` 66 times, `href="/services"` 59 times; only the guide cross-links use the slash form, and those are mixed (`/guides/cleaning-wool-carpets` twice and `/guides/cleaning-wool-carpets/` twice). Templates: `site/src/components/Nav.astro:8-16`, `site/src/components/Footer.astro:8`, `site/src/pages/index.astro:38-39,85-88,99,107`.
- **Fix.** Set `trailingSlash: 'always'` in `site/astro.config.mjs` (the installed Astro 5.18.2 supports it; the sitemap integration honours it), then sweep every internal `href` to the slash form. `Nav.astro:6-7` compares `path === href`, so its active-link check needs the same change. Add a build-time check so it cannot drift back: a `node --test` that reads `site/dist/**/index.html` and fails on any internal `href` without a trailing slash (the project's enforce-invariants-in-build rule).
- **Priority.** Before launch, or in the first week. Standard: Google Search Central, "Redirects and Google Search" and "Consolidate duplicate URLs".

### T-04. XML sitemap: present, correct scope, no lastmod (Amber, low effort)

- **Issue.** `sitemap-index.xml` and `sitemap-0.xml` are generated and list all 26 public pages with absolute `www` URLs, correctly excluding `/booking-action/` (`astro.config.mjs:12`). No entry carries `<lastmod>`.
- **Impact.** Modest. Google uses `lastmod` when it is accurate and consistent to prioritise recrawls of changed pages. Every guide and area page already stores an `updated` ISO date in frontmatter (`site/src/content.config.ts:16,43`), so the data exists and is currently discarded.
- **Evidence.** Built `site/dist/sitemap-0.xml`: 26 `<loc>` entries, zero `<lastmod>` elements.
- **Fix.** `@astrojs/sitemap` 3.7.3 (installed) exposes a `serialize(item)` hook that can set `item.lastmod`. Feed it the collection's `updated` date for `/guides/*` and `/areas/*`, and leave static pages without one rather than stamping the build date on everything (a `lastmod` that changes on every deploy is ignored).
- **Priority.** Quick win after launch. Standard: Google Search Central, "Build and submit a sitemap" (lastmod guidance).

### T-05. Duplicate URL forms answer 200 (Green)

- **Issue.** `/index.html` and `/about/index.html` return 200 alongside `/` and `/about/`.
- **Impact.** Negligible: each carries the self-referencing canonical to the slash form, and nothing links to the `index.html` forms.
- **Evidence.** Live probes, 14 September 2026.
- **Fix.** None required. If tidiness is wanted, a Netlify redirect from `/*/index.html` to `/*/` (301) closes it.

### T-06. robots.txt (Green)

`User-agent: *` / `Allow: /` plus the sitemap reference (`site/public/robots.txt:12-15`). Correct for the pre-launch design and correct after cutover. The file's own comment explains why `Disallow: /` would be the wrong pre-launch tool. Leave it.

### T-07. Operator utility page correctly excluded (Green)

`/booking-action/` carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `no-referrer` (`site/src/pages/booking-action.astro:24-25`) and is filtered from the sitemap. Nothing links to it. Correct.

## 4. Technical foundations

### T-08. HTTPS, redirects and security headers (Green)

- `http://` 301s to `https://` on the Netlify host. HSTS is served with `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload`; the `preload` token is not in the value set at `netlify.toml:128`, so Netlify's own HSTS setting is supplying it. `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` and a `Permissions-Policy` are all present (`netlify.toml:121-128`). No CSP, which the file explains is deferred until the inline handlers in `book.astro` and `admin.html` are refactored (`netlify.toml:118-120`).
- At cutover, confirm the same on the real domain and that the apex redirects to `www` (runbook checklist line, `docs/LAUNCH_CUTOVER.md:211`).

### T-09. No long-cache header on content-hashed assets (Amber, low effort)

- **Issue.** Astro emits hashed filenames under `/_astro/` (for example `/_astro/about.CrE_nXI8.css`, `/_astro/lato-latin-400-normal.BEhtfm5r.woff2`). Netlify serves them with `Cache-Control: public,max-age=0,must-revalidate`, so returning visitors revalidate every stylesheet and font on every page view.
- **Impact.** Small on first view (the CDN still caches at the edge), real on repeat views and across pages: the same fonts and CSS are re-validated on each navigation. Core Web Vitals field data is collected from real visits, so repeat-view cost counts.
- **Evidence.** Live header on `/_astro/about.CrE_nXI8.css`, 14 September 2026.
- **Fix.** Add to `netlify.toml`:

  ```toml
  [[headers]]
    for = "/_astro/*"
    [headers.values]
      Cache-Control = "public, max-age=31536000, immutable"
  ```

  Safe because the hash changes whenever the content does. `/logo.jpg` in `public/` is not hashed, so leave it on the default.
- **Priority.** Quick win. Standard: web.dev, "Use long cache TTLs for hashed assets".

### T-10. Page weight and rendering (Green, with one note)

- Content pages ship **no JavaScript**. The home page is one 25 KB stylesheet, a 12 KB WebP hero image (from a 428 KB PNG source, resized to three widths with explicit `width`/`height`, `loading="eager"` and `fetchpriority="high"`, `site/src/pages/index.astro:52-61`), and fonts.
- One in-app browser load of `/` on 14 September 2026 (not a measurement, one sample): `CLS 0` with zero layout-shift entries, `DOMContentLoaded` at 0.9 s, load event at 1.2 s, TTFB 0.7 s on a cold edge. LCP could not be read after the fact from that session.
- **Note on fonts.** The home page fetched eight font files totalling about 131 KB: Raleway 700 and 800, Lato 300/400/700 in `latin`, and the three Lato `latin-ext` faces as well (18 KB between them). The layout imports `latin-ext` for customer-typed names in the chat (`site/src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:16-20`), which only matters on `/book`. Two low-cost options: import the `latin-ext` faces only in `book.astro`, or accept the 18 KB. Neither is urgent.
- **Recommendation.** Run PageSpeed Insights on the Netlify host (it works on noindexed pages) once the anonymous quota resets, and again on the real domain after cutover. Thresholds: LCP under 2.5 s, INP under 200 ms, CLS under 0.1 (Core Web Vitals, per the skill).

### T-11. Mobile rendering (Green)

Viewport meta is set (`BaseLayout.astro:77`). At 375 px in the in-app browser, `/areas/cheltenham/` renders with no horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth` 375), the CSS-only burger menu replaces the link row, headings and the surcharge badge wrap cleanly, and all four JSON-LD blocks are present in the DOM. The nav checkbox pattern is keyboard-reachable and labelled (`Nav.astro:25-32`). No separate mobile site, same content as desktop.

### T-12. Custom 404 (Green, quick win)

`/nonexistent-page-xyz` returns 404 with Netlify's default "Page not found" page: no navigation, no brand, no way back. Add `site/src/pages/404.astro` through `BaseLayout`; Astro writes `404.html` and Netlify serves it with a 404 status automatically. Low priority, but it is a five-minute job and every mistyped URL currently dead-ends.

### T-13. `lang="en"` (Green)

`BaseLayout.astro:74` declares `en`. Google reads the visible content to determine language and region; Bing gives weight to the attribute. `en-GB` is the more precise value for a UK-only service and costs nothing. Optional.

### T-14. Favicon is a 900 x 491 JPEG (Amber, quick win)

- **Issue.** `<link rel="icon" type="image/jpeg" href="/logo.jpg">` (`BaseLayout.astro:81`) points at the 900 x 491 px logo.
- **Impact.** Google shows a site's favicon beside its results on mobile and desktop. Its guideline is that the favicon must be square and a multiple of 48 px (48, 96, 144 and so on). A non-square 900 x 491 image may be ignored, and the browser tab icon is a squashed logo.
- **Fix.** Export a square icon (a 96 x 96 or 192 x 192 PNG cropped from the logo mark, plus a 32 x 32 `favicon.ico`), add an `apple-touch-icon` (180 x 180), and keep `/logo.jpg` for Open Graph. Source: Google Search Central, "Define a favicon to show in search results".

### T-15. Open Graph and Twitter cards (Green)

Every page emits `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:type`, `og:url`, `og:image`, `og:site_name` and the Twitter `summary_large_image` set (`BaseLayout.astro:82-91`). The shared image is the 900 x 491 logo, which meets the large-card minimum (600 x 315). Page-specific images would improve shares of the guides but there are no page images to use yet; not a gap worth chasing before there is real content photography.

## 5. On-page

All 26 public pages have a unique `<title>`, a unique `<meta name="description">`, a self-referencing canonical and exactly one `<h1>` (verified in the build). The findings below are about length and wording, not presence.

### O-01. Home H1 carries no service or place term (Amber)

- **Issue.** `<h1>` is "Smarter Cleaning. Done Properly." (`site/src/pages/index.astro:35`). The primary term for the site, carpet cleaning in Cheltenham and Gloucestershire, appears in the title and the sub-line but not the heading.
- **Impact.** The H1 is one of the stronger on-page relevance signals and the home page is the page most likely to rank for the head term.
- **Fix.** Keep the strapline as the visual hero if the brand wants it, but make the H1 the descriptive line, for example "Carpet Cleaning in Cheltenham and Gloucestershire, Done Properly", and demote "Smarter Cleaning." to styled text. Owner call on the wording; the point is that the H1 should say what the page is.
- Related, lower weight: `/areas/` H1 "Where We Clean" (`areas/index.astro:41`) and `/contact/` H1 "Contact Us" (`contact.astro:18`) would both carry more with the service term ("Carpet Cleaning Service Areas in Gloucestershire", "Contact Intelligent Carpet Cleaning, Cheltenham"). `/book/` H1 "AI Cleaning Assistant" (`book.astro:25`) is fine for a tool page.

### O-02. Titles over the working limit (Amber, quick win)

Working limit from the skill: 50 to 60 characters visible in results (Google truncates by pixel width, so this is a guide, not a rule). Measured from the build:

| Page | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| `/services/decontamination-fogging/` | 85 | Decontamination & Fogging, Cheltenham & Gloucestershire \| Intelligent Carpet Cleaning |
| `/services/pressure-washing/` | 76 | Pressure Washing, Cheltenham & Gloucestershire \| Intelligent Carpet Cleaning |
| `/guides/natural-fibre-carpets/` | 69 | Cleaning Natural-Fibre Carpets and Rugs \| Intelligent Carpet Cleaning |

Options: shorten the suffix on guides to " | ICC Guides" or drop it on long titles (Google now shows the site name separately above each result, so the suffix does less work than it used to), and cut the two specialist titles to "Decontamination & Fogging in Gloucestershire | Intelligent Carpet Cleaning" (72) or "Ozone Decontamination & Fogging, Gloucestershire" without the suffix. Sources: `guides/[slug].astro:71` (suffix), `services/decontamination-fogging.astro:48`, `services/pressure-washing.astro:44`, `services.astro:14`.

The home title, "Intelligent Carpet Cleaning | Cheltenham & Gloucestershire" (58), is brand-first; the brand contains the head term, so it works. Leave it.

### O-03. Descriptions over the working limit (Green, quick win)

Working limit 150 to 160 characters. Over: `/services/decontamination-fogging/` at 222 (`decontamination-fogging.astro:49`), `/terms/` at 186 (`terms.astro:19`), `/services/pressure-washing/` at 177 (`pressure-washing.astro:45`), `/guides/diy-vs-professional-carpet-cleaning/` at 164. ### O-04. Heading structure (Green)

One H1 per page, H2 sections, H3 inside cards and FAQs; no skipped levels found in the templates or the Markdown guides (all guide bodies use `##` only). The FAQ questions render as H3 under an H2 (`guides/[slug].astro:91-97`, `areas/[slug].astro:131-139`), which matches the JSON-LD.

### O-05. Internal linking: two near-orphans and weak specialist-page links (Amber)

- `/history/` receives one internal link on the whole site, a text link at the foot of the guides index (`guides/index.astro:33`). It is an expert article of about 940 words and it is not in the guides collection, the navigation, or the home page.
- `/services/decontamination-fogging/` and `/services/pressure-washing/` each receive exactly one link, from the card on `/services/` (`services.astro:118,124`). Nothing on the home page, the area pages or the footer mentions them, so their only crawl path is one click deep off a secondary page. These are the two pages the business would most like to be found for outside carpet cleaning (D-038).
- The area pages link to `/services`, `/book` and `/areas` but not to each other or to the guides they mention in prose (the FAQ answers say "See our wool-carpet guide" as plain text, `content/areas/cheltenham.md`, with no link because FAQ answers are rendered as text).
- **Fix.** Add `/history/` to the guides index as a card or move it into the collection; add a "Specialist services" line to the footer or the home services grid; add a "Related guides" list of two or three links to the area template. Anchor text already descriptive throughout. No broken internal links were found: every `href` in the build resolves to a built page.

### O-06. Images and alt text (Green)

Two `<img>` elements on the site: the chat avatar (`alt="ICC"`, `book.astro:42`) and the hidden upload preview (`alt=""`, correct for decorative). The hero uses `<Image>` with `alt="Intelligent Carpet Cleaning"` (`index.astro:54`). Everything else is inline SVG with `aria-hidden` (`Icon.astro:49`). Nothing to fix. There is no photography on the site at all, which is a content observation rather than an SEO defect (section 8).

### O-07. Keyword targeting and cannibalisation (Green)

Each area page targets "carpet cleaning in {town}" in title, H1 and URL and nothing else does. Guides each own a distinct question. `/services/` and the home page both target the head term, which is normal: the home page for the brand-plus-place query, `/services/` for pricing and method queries. No two pages compete for the same title.

### O-08. House style: em dashes in visible copy (Green, style)

The project's house style bans em dashes (CLAUDE.md, the GBP spec, and the assistant prompt itself). The site copy still uses them: in the area badges and headings (`areas/[slug].astro:88,90,132`, `areas/index.astro:42,46,60`), the footer (`Footer.astro:7`), the contact page, the history page, and 13 FAQ answers across the area and guide frontmatter, which are also emitted verbatim into the `FAQPage` JSON-LD. No ranking effect; consistency with the assistant's own register and the "no em dash" rule. Fix with the copy pass, not as an SEO task.

### S-01. `Article` blocks lack `image` and `datePublished` (Amber)

- **Issue.** `guides/[slug].astro:30-44` emits `headline`, `description`, `url`, `mainEntityOfPage`, `dateModified`, `author` (Organization) and `publisher`. No `image`, no `datePublished`.
- **Impact.** The `schema` skill's own table lists `headline`, `image`, `datePublished` and `author` for Article. Google's current documentation treats all Article properties as recommended rather than required, so Search Console will report the two missing ones as warnings, not errors, and Article rich results are rare for a non-news site in any case. The gap costs little in ranking terms and is cheap to close.
- **Fix.** Add `published` to the guides collection schema (or reuse `updated` as `datePublished` for the first release and keep both fields thereafter) and emit it; add `image` pointing at `/logo.jpg` until the guides have their own images. Source: Google Search Central, "Article structured data".

### S-02. `BreadcrumbList` missing on the specialist service pages (Amber, quick win)

The two pages under `/services/` are two levels deep like the area and guide pages, but emit no breadcrumb block (`decontamination-fogging.astro:13-22`, `pressure-washing.astro:9-18` carry only `Service`). Copy the three-item pattern from `areas/[slug].astro:33-41` (Home, Services, page). Top-level pages do not need one.

### S-03. Breadcrumb markup without a full visible trail (Green)

Google asks that breadcrumb markup reflect breadcrumbs shown on the page. Area and guide pages show only the parent label as a link ("Service Areas", "Care Guides") above the H1 (`areas/[slug].astro:85`, `guides/[slug].astro:80`), not the Home link the markup declares. A small visible trail (Home / Service Areas / Cheltenham) closes the gap and doubles as navigation. Low priority.

### S-04. `FAQPage`: keep it, but do not expect rich results (Green, expectation-setting)

In August 2023 Google announced it was limiting FAQ rich results to well-known, authoritative government and health sites. The markup here will not produce the expandable Q&A in results. It still describes the page accurately for search engines and for AI answer engines, which read structured Q&A readily, so keep it. Just do not list "FAQ rich results" as a launch metric.
